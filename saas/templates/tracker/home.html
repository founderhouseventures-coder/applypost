Questions, answered

Do you read my email?

No. You add only the job details you choose. There is no inbox connection.

Can I bring my spreadsheet?

Yes. Import a CSV, preview it, then confirm. You can export everything back anytime.

What does resume version tracking do?

Tag each application with the resume you sent, then see which version gets more interviews.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Account deletion removes your applications and account in one step.

Privacy, in plain language

JobMail stores the application details you add to your account so it can show your tracker. It does not request Gmail access, scan inboxes, sell personal data, or store email bodies. You can export your application list or delete your account from the dashboard.

Beta terms: JobMail is an organizational tool, not an employment agency. It does not guarantee interviews, offers, or employment. Use the beta at your discretion and keep your own copies of important application records.
