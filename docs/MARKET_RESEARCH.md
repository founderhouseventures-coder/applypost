# JobMail market research

**Checked:** 2026-11-17. Public prices are USD unless noted and can vary by checkout, region, and promotion. This is desk research, not customer validation. Review observations below are individual or coded public-user comments; none is a claim about every customer.

## Target and problem hypothesis

Target: job seekers who receive application, interview, and offer updates by email and want those milestones in a private, spreadsheet-owned tracker. JobMail locally reads Gmail and can append selected details to the user's Google Sheet. It is not an auto-apply service and does not promise interviews. The hypotheses to validate are missed updates, repeated manual entry, and a preference for local control over a hosted job-search account.

## Positioning hypothesis

**JobMail turns opted-in Gmail job updates into a private, spreadsheet-owned timeline, with a read-only preview before any write and no inbox-body storage by default.** Compete on local control, traceable writes, and reversible setup—not on application volume, resume scores, or guaranteed outcomes. The product is currently a local automation plus a static demo; a one-off guided setup/support offer is more credible than a subscription until buyers validate recurring use.

## Buyer questions

1. Which job updates do people currently re-enter, miss, or lose?
2. Would they grant Gmail read-only permission for a local preview? Which scope or storage behavior would stop them?
3. Is Google Sheets a benefit, or does setup make it too difficult?
4. Would they pay for guided OAuth/filter setup, and what deliverable would justify the fee?
5. Which messages or fields must never leave the inbox?

## Recommendation and limits

Validate a fixed-scope, human-reviewed setup session with job seekers or career centers before adding hosted accounts or billing. Keep email bodies off, require the user to preview matches, and make no automatic application or outreach claims. Competitor prices and reviews should be refreshed before a public sales page; buyer demand, conversion, and revenue are unverified.
